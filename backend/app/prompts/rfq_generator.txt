Draft a formal, execution-ready Request for Quotation (RFQ) for an Indian manufacturer.
Include:
- Product requirements and technical specifications
- Quantity and MOQ request
- Material and quality tolerances
- Packaging and labelling specifications
- Required certifications (e.g., BIS, ISO 9001, FSSAI, FSC)
- Delivery deadline (in days) and destination (e.g., Bengaluru, Karnataka)
- Itemized pricing request in INR (₹) including ex-works unit cost, packaging, GST breakdown, and surface freight
- Payment terms and sample dispatch schedule
